02The idea

Two lines, one point, four colours

Two straight lines through one point — the fold line and the arm’s edge — cut the K into four colours that meet at the junction J. Nothing is added to the letter; the colours are its own construction.

05Clear space and minimum size

Room to breathe, sizes that hold

Clear space keeps the mark legible; minimum sizes keep its construction visible.

Clear space

Keep one stem width, w, free on every side of the mark’s bounds: no text, edges, images or other marks inside the tinted zone. On the badge, the disc is the clear space.

Minimum sizes

Screen

16 px high: the colour mark, the badge and the solid one-colour mark. The favicons are grid-fitted to hold it.

Print

Mark height at least 8 mm; 20 mm for the gapped one-colour mark. Shown here at 8 mm for a print at 100%.

One colour

The gap is 0.8% of the mark height: gapped from 128 px (a 1 px gap) and 20 mm. Smaller, use the solid one-colour mark.

Blue is outside the CMYK gamut (ΔE 31.7). For print, match it with a spot ink chosen against a physical swatch book, and approve it on a proof on the real stock. Green is just outside the gamut (ΔE 9.2): check it on the proof too.

08Backgrounds

Where the mark sits

Contrast first: the four colours need a calm, light or dark field to read as one letter.

Light

Full colour on white and light neutrals. The premium shaded mark is made for light backgrounds too.

Dark

On night or other dark fields: the full-colour mark, or the white one-colour mark with gaps.

Photography

Use the badge: its white disc keeps every colour true on any image. Never the bare mark on a photo.

Yellow

Brand yellow never stands alone on white (1.67:1): it appears only inside the mark.

10App icons and favicons

Icons for every screen

The tech icon for apps, the badge for avatars and the bare, grid-fitted K for favicons.

Tech icon

Rounded tile for apps and docks; the full-bleed square for stores, which apply their own mask. Below 120 px, the small version: no grid, traces or node.

Small version, px

Android adaptive

The tech icon at 2/3 scale, so the 72 dp view (dashed) frames it like the 1024 icon. Background: plate, grid, light; foreground: traces, lit K, node, the K inside the 66 dp circle (solid). Themed: the solid K.

Favicon

Grid-fitted: cap and base on pixel rows, stem on whole pixels. Shown at 1× and enlarged, pixel for pixel as shipped.

1×: 16 and 32 px

Avatar badge

Upload the square; the platform’s circle crop lands on the disc edge and the K stays whole.

11File guide

What’s in the kit

Pick the folder by the program you work in. Never redraw the artwork: change brand.py and rebuild.

For native Illustrator layers and global swatches, run logo/scripts/K-Logo_Illustrator.jsx inside Illustrator (File > Scripts > Other Script…).
